# Patchwell: business plan

**Accessibility audits with paste-in code fixes for small online stores. Flat $79 / $149 / $399.**
Goal: earn at least enough to cover the Claude subscription (about $20–$200/month), then grow.
That works out to **1–2 Fix Reports a month**.

## Why this business (research, Oct 2026)
I rejected the first idea (a daily puzzle game) because the market is saturated and people won't pay for it. I compared these options:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Etsy/Gumroad digital products | Saturated. One report claims 44% of Gumroad products earn $0. |
| Websites for local businesses | Plenty of demand, but lots of agencies, buyers are price-sensitive, and sales is hard |
| Generic AI micro-SaaS | "50 competitors before your first customer"; needs a backend and support |
| **Accessibility audits with code fixes** | **Demand is driven by law, the cheap option has been discredited, there's a price gap, and it's work I can do well** |

**Demand is driven by law and growing:**
- About 3,100–3,950 US federal ADA website suits were filed in 2025, up 24–27% on 2024. Around 6,000 digital accessibility suits are projected for 2026. About 69% target e-commerce, mostly small and mid-size companies.
- The European Accessibility Act has applied to consumer e-commerce since 28 June 2025 (micro-enterprises are exempt).
- Small-business settlements usually run $5k–$20k, so a $149 fix report is cheap protection and a good-faith signal. We never sell it as a guarantee.

**The cheap option has been discredited:**
- The FTC finalised a $1M order against accessiBe (Apr 2025) for claiming its widget made sites compliant.
- Even so, 25 of the 28 apps in Shopify's accessibility category are overlays. About 1 in 4 new lawsuits name sites that already had an overlay installed.

**There's a price gap:**
| Competitor | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Fiverr gigs | $40–$50 | Mostly an automated scan exported to PDF, no fixes |
| Overlay widgets | $490–$3,990/yr | A toolbar, no code fixes |
| Code-fix SaaS (e.g. Fudge.ai) | $599/mo | Strong, but priced out of reach for small stores |
| Agency manual audit | $1,500–$7,000 | A report; remediation billed separately and costs more |
| **Patchwell** | **$79–$399 once** | **Report plus paste-ready fixes plus a re-check** |

**Why I can win it:** the work is reading code, running tests and writing fixes, which is exactly what I'm good at, and my labour cost is close to zero. So I can sell at $149 something that agencies charge $1.5k+ for, and still deliver more than the $50 scans.

## Products
- **Quick Audit, $79:** 3 pages, ranked report
- **Fix Report, $149:** 10 pages, code fixes, free re-check *(the main product)*
- **Done For You, $399:** we apply the fixes to an unpublished copy of the theme
- **Monthly Re-check, $19/mo:** recurring revenue add-on

## Getting customers (in order of speed)
1. **Fiverr and Upwork gigs (weeks 1–2).** Buyers there already search "accessibility audit". I'll price above the $50 scans and win on fixes and sample quality. Listing copy is in `ops/GIG.md`.
2. **Free bookmarklet checker (ongoing).** It finds problems on a store's own page and links to pricing. Post it on r/shopify, r/smallbusiness, r/ecommerce, Indie Hackers and Shopify Community (as a helpful tool, not spam).
3. **Warm, value-first outreach (week 3+).** Find small stores with real issues and send a free 3-issue mini report. Only to business contact addresses, CAN-SPAM compliant, with an easy opt-out, at low volume.
4. **SEO pages:** "How to fix [common issue] in Shopify Dawn" articles. Content is my strength.

## Unit economics
Costs: domain about $12/yr, hosting $0 (GitHub Pages), Stripe 2.9% + 30¢, Fiverr takes 20%.
A $149 Fix Report nets about $141 via Stripe, or $119 via Fiverr.
**Break-even: 2 Quick Audits or 1 Fix Report a month.**

| | Month 1 | Month 3 | Month 6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders (mostly Fiverr at first) | 1–2 | 4–6 | 8–12 |
| Revenue | $80–$230 | $500–$800 | $1,200–$2,000 |

These are targets, not promises. Treat month 1 as validation.

## Ethics and risk rules I hold myself to
- I never claim "ADA compliant", "lawsuit-proof" or "certified".
- I never use fear-based cold outreach or reference specific lawsuits against a prospect.
- I say openly that delivery is AI-assisted. Every report is reviewed before it's sent.
- Customers who already have a demand letter are told to see a lawyer first.
- The refund guarantee is a full refund if the report has fewer than 5 actionable fixes.
