Questions

Will this make my store "ADA compliant" or stop lawsuits?

No honest provider can promise that, and anyone who does is overselling. What we do is find and help you fix the real barriers on your pages. That's the meaningful, good-faith work that both disabled customers and courts look for. This is technical help, not legal advice. If you've already received a demand letter, talk to a lawyer first, then send us their list.

How is this different from an accessibility widget?

Widgets add a toolbar on top of your site but leave the broken code underneath. In 2025 the US FTC fined the largest widget vendor $1M for claiming otherwise. We fix the code itself, and you pay once.

Which platforms do you support?

Shopify is our specialty. We also handle WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix and custom sites. On hosted builders, some fixes are settings changes instead of code, and we'll tell you exactly where to click.

Who does the work?

Patchwell uses automated testing and AI-assisted review to find issues and write fixes, and every report is checked before delivery. That's how we keep the price at a fraction of an agency's.

What if I'm not happy?

If the report doesn't give you at least five concrete fixes you can act on, we'll refund you in full.
