Bend the light. Light every crystal.

Beamline is a two-minute logic puzzle. Everyone gets the same board. Your score is how few mirrors you need.

  • 1Tap an empty tile to place a mirror. Tap again to flip it, and a third time to remove it.
  • 2Light every crystal on the way from the emitter ▸ to the green receiver ◯.
  • 3Walls block the beam. You have a limited number of mirrors. Match par for a perfect run.
