# What I need you to do (about 1–2 hours total)

I run the business: auditing, writing fixes, writing reports, marketing copy, the site, and replies to customers that you paste in.
You're the legal and financial "hands", because an AI can't hold accounts or receive money.

## Required to take the first order
1. **Fiverr seller account** (fastest route to first revenue). Paste the gig from `ops/GIG.md`, then upload `samples/sample-report.html` (as a PDF or screenshots) as the gig's portfolio sample. Fiverr requires you to say that AI is used. The gig copy already does.
2. **Business email.** A free Proton or Gmail address like `patchwell.audits@...` is fine to start.
3. **Turn on GitHub Pages** for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch `claude/accessibility-business`, or merge it to main). The current `tusk-pages.yml` workflow is for the old Tusk site, so disable or delete it.
4. **Put the email address in `CONFIG.contactEmail`** in `index.html`, or just tell me and I'll do it.

## Within the first month
5. **Stripe account** (individual/sole proprietor is fine). Create 3 Payment Links ($79, $149, $399) and send me the URLs. I'll wire them in.
6. **Domain** (about $12/yr), for example patchwell-audits.com. Check the name is free first (USPTO search plus a quick Google). Tell me if it's taken and I'll rename everything.
7. **Taxes.** Keep a note of income. Sole-proprietor income goes on your normal return. Ask an accountant if unsure.

## The loop when an order comes in
1. Paste the order details (store URL and pages) into a Claude Code session on this repo and say "new order".
2. I run the audit, do the manual review, write the fixes, and produce `reports/<client>/report.html`.
3. You glance at it, export it to PDF (Ctrl+P), and deliver it on Fiverr or by email.
4. Paste any customer questions to me and I'll draft the replies.

Your time per order is about 10 minutes.
