Questions

What ages is it for?

Kids from 3 to 12. The charts come in two versions (3–7 and 8–12), and the parent guide has suggestions for each age group.

Is this a physical product?

No, it's an instant digital download (PDF). You print the pages you want at home or at a print shop, as many times as you like, for your own family.

Does it work on A4 paper?

Yes. The pages are US Letter size and print fine on A4 with "fit to page".

Do I have to ban screens completely?

Not at all. The goal is balance: screens at agreed times, without the daily fight. Your family sets its own limits in the agreement.

What if my kids push back hard?

They probably will, for the first few days. That's normal, and the parent guide includes calm, word-for-word phrases for exactly those moments.

Is this medical or therapy advice?

No. It's a practical parenting toolkit with general ideas. If you're worried about your child's wellbeing or development, please talk to your pediatrician.
